Analyze the actual job before drafting. Infer company and role from supplied job information if optional fields are empty. Identify responsibilities, seniority, mandatory/preferred requirements, ATS terms, and tailoring strategy. Map each meaningful requirement to eligible fact_ids: direct_match, transferable_match, weak_match or no_evidence. Related tools are not direct experience. Explain distinctions. no_evidence must have no fact_ids. Include gaps and dangerous claims. Do not identify requirements by capitalization. Employer claims need support in the job text. Notes guide emphasis but never add evidence.

Distinguish university coursework from professional practice. Transcript evidence can directly support an academic-knowledge requirement; for a requirement for practical or industrial experience it is at most transferable/weak evidence unless other facts establish that practice.
